How it works

1. Pick the cut

Brisket, pork shoulder, lamb leg, spatchcock chicken and more.

2. Pick the cooker

Smoker, kettle, gas or oven. Each has its own pit temperature.

3. Enter the weight

Kilograms only. The plan scales with what is on the board.

4. Get the plan

Cook time, pull temp, rest, and the time to light the fire.

5. Work backwards

Set the time you want to eat and it tells you when to start.

6. Save it

Keep the plan on your phone for the next cook.
